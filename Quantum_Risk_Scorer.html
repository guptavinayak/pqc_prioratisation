Audit trail — why this number

    The engine, explained — five factors, one formula, four waves
    Core model

    Risk = Impact × Likelihood

    Impact is built from five weighted categories (weights sum to 1.0). Likelihood is a threat multiplier applied after impact: public-facing ×1.30, internal-only ×1.00. The final score maps to four migration waves.

    CIA is applied consistently

    Every risk-relevant factor is judged across Confidentiality 0.42, Integrity 0.42, Availability 0.16. A leaf weight is always:

    category × sub-category × CIA

    e.g. 0.23 × 0.50 × 0.42 = 0.0483 for customer detriment on confidentiality.
