Audit trail — why this number

    The engine, explained — five factors, one formula, three bands
    Core model

    Raw = Σ (weight × score)

    Display Score = (Raw ÷ 1.0) × 100. The denominator is 1.0 because the weights themselves sum to 1.0 — not an invented ceiling. There are no post-hoc multipliers. Exposure is argued inside the factor scores, where it is visible and challengeable.

    Bands carry deadlines in writing, never labels alone. The Low band starts at 14.5, not 0: the ladder's floor rung is Minor, so the lowest score the model can produce is 0.145 → 14.5. No rated asset is ever scored risk-free.

    Exact ties are settled by the exposure rule: on an identical score, the public-facing application queues first. Public reach already earns a True Blast Radius inside the score; the exposure rule resolves the residual tie when Blast Radius and Data Sensitivity trade places.

    Why it is explainable

    Every score traces to a factor. Every weight is explicit in one configuration object. The same logic drives the UI, bulk scoring, and reporting. Because there is no multiplier layer, the number you see is the number a regulator can recompute.
